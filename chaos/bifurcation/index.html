About this map

How to read and use it

Orbit: the first 120 iterates from x₀; the shaded band is the transient that the bifurcation diagram discards.

Cobweb: the graph of fk against the diagonal; each step goes up to the curve and across to the diagonal. Fixed points of fk (period-k points) are marked: filled = stable (|(fk)′| < 1), hollow = unstable.

Bifurcation diagram: for every parameter value, the iterates left after the transient, shaded by how often they land on each pixel. Drag a box to zoom (a horizontal drag zooms the parameter only); Back undoes a zoom. Self-similar copies of the whole diagram appear inside every periodic window.

Lyapunov exponent: λ = lim (1/n) Σ ln |f′(xi)|, the average exponential rate at which nearby orbits separate. It touches 0 at every bifurcation and dives to −∞ at superstable cycles.

Sharing: the address bar keeps the map, parameters and zoom, so “Copy link” reproduces the exact view.
